Parchment band

The classic hello bar, inverted: a warm Parchment band with Obsidian text, so it is the one light thing at the top of a dark room. A small seal-style “New” mark, the update, and the date right-aligned. Most noticeable of the five.

  • The message is real: the most recent update shipped on Oct 4, 2026 (commit 6cd1e64). It will live as one editable entry in lib/site.ts.
  • × dismisses it (try it). In the build, dismissal is remembered for that update only, so the next update shows again.
  • While it shows, the wall label and the painting's top overlays move down by the bar's height so nothing is covered.
